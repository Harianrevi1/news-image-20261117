## The image

One front page, 17 November 2026, in four panels:

1. **Ukraine hits the Crimea Bridge again** — a second Ukrainian strike on the
   Kerch Bridge in under 24 hours; traffic suspended, while Russian attacks
   hit Kyiv's river crossings.
2. **Iran drones target a U.S. base** — Iran fired drones at the U.S. military
   radar system at Sheikh Isa base in Bahrain (intercepted, no casualties),
   and tankers were hit by mines near the Strait of Hormuz.
3. **Xi, Trump, Putin to meet** — China confirmed the first three-way meeting
   of the three leaders, set for Thursday in Shenzhen on the APEC sidelines;
   Trump arrived in Manila for the ASEAN summit ahead of it.
4. **Japan scrambles fighter jets** — Japan launched jets and protested after
   two suspected Russian aircraft violated its airspace early in the morning.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the bundled
`entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`. The program runs with no network, so the
image ships in the repository and the output is byte-identical every run.
